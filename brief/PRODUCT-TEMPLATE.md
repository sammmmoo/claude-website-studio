## Product Principles

1. Phone first: most visitors are on a phone, so the phone version is the most finished one.
2. The first screen says what we do, where, and how to get in touch.
3. One main action, the same all the way down the page.
4. Real proof only. Absent beats invented.
5. Fast on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Readable without zooming, tap targets of 44 px, everything readable with reduced motion or before JavaScript runs.
