# Website brief: example

A finished brief for a made-up business, so you can see what good answers look like. Northside Drains isn't real, and neither is its number (0491 570 110 is in the range set aside for fiction). Notice what's left OPEN: nothing gets invented to fill a gap.

## The business

- Business name: Northside Drains
- What you do, the way a customer would say it: We unblock drains, usually the same day.
- Main services, the one that earns you the most first: blocked drains and sewers; drain camera inspections; stormwater drains; hot water repairs and replacements; general plumbing repairs.
- Where you work: Brisbane's northside, from Chermside out to Redcliffe and across to Strathpine.
- Who calls you, and what's usually going on when they do: homeowners and property managers. Something's backing up or smells, they're stressed, often on their phone outside, and they want someone today.
- Why people pick you over the next business: we put a camera down the drain before quoting, so we fix the cause, not just the blockage. Fixed quote before we start. Licensed plumbers, not labourers.

## Enquiries

- The main action you want: a call. A quote form for people who can't talk.
- Phone number for tap-to-call: 0491 570 110
- Email: hello@example.com
- Hours, and after-hours work: 7am to 5pm Monday to Saturday. After-hours callouts for blocked sewers only.
- Where form enquiries should go: our email for now. OPEN: we might move to a CRM later.
- What happens after someone enquires, and how fast: we call back within the hour in business hours, otherwise first thing next morning.

## Proof (real only)

- Google reviews to use: OPEN. Owner to send three Google reviews word for word, with first name and suburb. Until then the reviews screen shows `[ADD: a real Google review]`.
- Photos: OPEN. We have a few phone photos of jobs; owner to send the best five. No stock photos pretending to be our work.
- Licences, insurance and memberships: OPEN. Owner to send the plumbing licence number.
- Years in business or jobs done: leave off for now.
- A guarantee you will honour: if the same drain blocks again within 30 days, we come back free. (Owner confirmed they'll honour it.)

## Look

- Logo file: none yet. Use the business name as a clean wordmark.
- Brand colours: the van is dark green and white. Use those.
- Sites you like or hate: hates sites with a big stock photo of a smiling plumber and a thumbs up.
- Must or must never be on the site: must show the phone number at the top. Never "cheapest in Brisbane".

## Pages

- Home: always
- A page for each main service: yes, blocked drains, camera inspections and hot water.
- About page: yes, short. Two brothers, started after ten years working for a big plumbing company. (Owner's words.)
- Privacy page: always
- Thank-you page after the form: no, a message on the page is fine.
- Domain: OPEN, owner is checking what's available.
- Hosting: OPEN.
